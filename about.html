Learn About Me!

I am a Russian-speaking artist based in New York City, where I draw inspiration from the city's diverse cultures, creative communities, and vibrant artistic environment.

My background and experiences have shaped my curiosity about the world and my interest in exploring different forms of creative expression.

I am passionate about combining personal perspectives with visual storytelling to communicate ideas and connect with others.

As a graphic designer, I enjoy experimenting with typography, composition, color, and visual storytelling to create engaging and meaningful designs.

My work explores the relationship between handmade elements and digital techniques, combining traditional artistic processes with modern technology.

I am interested in developing creative solutions while continuing to expand my skills in graphic design, motion graphics, and digital media.
